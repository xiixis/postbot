Privacy Policy

4xTube Publisher · last updated 5 September 2026

4xTube Publisher is a private publishing tool. It prepares and posts content to social accounts owned by its single operator. It is not offered to the public, has no user sign-ups, and serves no visitors other than that operator.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (username, display name, and the privacy options the account allows), requested through the user.info.basic scope. It is used to show which account is connected and to pick a permitted post visibility.
  • Publishing permission for that same account, through the video.publish scope, used only to create posts the operator has approved.

What the app stores

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the connected account, kept so the app does not have to ask for permission again on every post.
  • The content the operator created: text, images, and the identifiers TikTok returns for published posts.

Tokens and content are stored in a private database that only the operator can reach. Nothing is stored about TikTok viewers, followers, or any other person.

What the app never does

  • It does not collect data about anyone other than the connected account.
  • It does not sell, rent, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or analytics.
  • It does not post anything the operator has not approved.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the account or revokes access in TikTok settings, after which they stop working and are deleted. Content is kept only while it is needed for publishing and is removed afterwards. To have everything erased, use the contact below.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
